# Cognitive Warfare Context Brief

**Purpose**: Internal strategic context for positioning and messaging.

## Historical Overview

Cognitive warfare is the modern evolution of psychological operations, propaganda, and information warfare. While the term is relatively new (gaining prominence in the 2010s–2020s), the underlying practice of targeting human perception and decision-making is ancient (Sun Tzu’s “win without fighting”).

Key historical phases:
- Ancient & Classical: Deception and psychological manipulation (Sun Tzu, Trojan Horse).
- 20th Century: Industrial-scale propaganda and psychological warfare (WW1, WW2, Cold War).
- Late 20th Century: Soviet “active measures” and “reflexive control” theory.
- 21st Century: Technological amplification via social media, AI, and data targeting. Formalization of “cognitive domain” concepts by NATO, China, and Russia.

## Current Landscape

- **China**: Advanced doctrine around “Cognitive Domain Operations” and integration with “intelligentized warfare.” Strong emphasis on the brain/mind as a battlespace. Heavy focus on influence operations (especially Taiwan).
- **Russia**: Builds on reflexive control. Often aims to create confusion and erode trust rather than win specific beliefs.
- **NATO/West**: More defensive framing — protecting populations and decision-makers from cognitive attacks. Growing recognition of the cognitive domain as a contested space.

## Relevance to IntelWar

Cognitive warfare makes the case for IntelWar’s existence clearer:

- The information environment is not neutral.
- Actors are actively targeting cognition at scale.
- There is a need for infrastructure that strengthens rigorous reasoning, provenance, and resistance to manipulation.
- The Living Log and constitutional model can be positioned as a form of **cognitive defense infrastructure** — a system that makes high-quality strategic thought harder to corrupt or erase.

This context should inform all external messaging without becoming the dominant public narrative (we lead with the positive vision of rigorous intellectual combat and compounding memory).
