# Reputation Mechanics for IntelWar
**Exploration + Concrete Proposal · 2026-07-19**

## Core question
> Whose contributions and judgment have consistently improved the quality of the shared strategic record?

Not popularity. Not social credit. Not orthodoxy enforcement.

## Failure modes to resist
1. **Popularity capture** — emotional / tribal resonance
2. **Compliance capture** — alignment with institutional preference over epistemic quality

## Domain ownership
Canonical reputation UI and ledgers live on **intelwar.net** (operational social + Log).  
`.org` may show lightweight merit signals only.

## Concrete architecture (v1)

### Multi-dimensional standing (Direction A)
Dimensions (each 0–10000 bps):
| Dimension | Primary inputs |
|-----------|----------------|
| Evidence Quality | Log persistence, cited evidence work |
| Adversarial Rigor | Survival under structured challenge |
| Cross-Check Quality | Error-finding / missing-context skill |
| Synthesis | Clarification and integration of contested lines |
| Judgment Reliability | Weighted peer attestation + update-under-pressure |

Composite core merit requires **convergence** — no single dimension may exceed 40% of composite weight.

### Contribution graph (Direction B)
Edges: `builds_on` | `cites` | `challenges`  
Inbound edges from high-standing nodes boost Log Persistence / Judgment — not raw volume.

### Stake + attestation (Direction C)
High-standing participants may stake standing when endorsing or challenging.  
Failed or careless stakes decay the staker. Creates cost for tribal endorsement rings.

### Visibility (middle path)
- Public: band + contextual dimension strengths (qualitative) + inspectable basis
- No public global leaderboard ordering
- Exact bps available on self-inspect / basis panel
- Work in the Living Log remains the final authority over any score

### Speed vs rigor
Fast signals (views, reactions, emotional velocity): **weight 0** in core merit.  
Slow signals dominate.

### Open questions (provisional answers)
1. Correct-over-time vs improving-the-contest: both count; survival + synthesis rewarded even when revised.
2. Penalties: relative neglect + stake loss preferred over public scarlet letters; repeated low-integrity can apply slow negative trajectory.
3. Transparency: basis inspectable; formula documented; no opaque black box.
4. Human + AI: AI proposes tags/signals; high tiers require human/hybrid review.
5. Pseudonymity: accountability-bound identities preferred for stake; pure anonymity cannot accrue Architect-band standing.

## Provisional stance
Reputation is a slow, multi-dimensional, hard-to-game record of contribution to the shared intellectual record. Subordinate to the Living Log content itself.
