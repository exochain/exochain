# IntelWar Social Layer — First-Principles Design
**Archived 2026-07-19 · Foundational stance for implementation**

## First principle
The primary purpose of social structure is to support rigorous intellectual combat, high-signal collaboration, and long-term accumulation of strategic knowledge — while still satisfying real human needs for recognition, alliance, and belonging.

## Principles
1. Merit before visibility
2. Context is scarce and valuable
3. Coalitions over permanent tribes
4. Status legible but hard to fake
5. Social features downstream of the intellectual system
6. Minimize user-as-product

## Rejected
Engagement optimization · Followers as status · Algorithmic reactivity amplification · Social graph as core product · Extractive monetization · Pure anonymity without accountability AND pure real-name chilling

## Structural direction
Reputation earned in arena/Log/contests · No infinite engagement feed · Mission-based association · Graduated visibility · 0dentity portable merit

## Domain ownership
- **intelwar.org** — Theatre entrance only (no full social experience)
- **intelwar.net** — Operational social + reputation + Living Log
