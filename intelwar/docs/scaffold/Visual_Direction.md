# Visual Direction & SVG Explainer Framework

**Aesthetic Reference**: Harvey.ai-inspired — sophisticated, restrained, intellectually confident.  
**Primary Format**: Animated SVG explainers.  
**Target Audience**: Intellectuals and the intelligentsia.

## Overall Aesthetic

- **Feel**: Quiet confidence. High-end research institute meets refined product design.
- **Typography**: Strong, elegant sans-serif. Excellent readability. Generous spacing.
- **Color Palette**: Restrained. Deep charcoals, off-whites, subtle accent (deep indigo or muted teal). High contrast.
- **Motion Philosophy**: Subtle, purposeful, revealing. Animations should clarify structure, relationships, or processes. Never decorative or flashy.
- **SVG Style**: Clean line work, minimal fills, elegant layering. Precision over ornamentation.

## Animated SVG Explainer Principles

- Length: Ideally 45–90 seconds.
- Focus on revealing systems and relationships rather than selling.
- Use motion to show process (e.g., consent flow → Log entry, multi-intelligence contest).
- Maintain intellectual dignity. Avoid cartoonish or overly corporate animation styles.
- Prioritize clarity and elegance.

## Recommended First Explainers (Priority Order)

1. **What is the Living Log?** — Core concept explanation.
2. **Intellectual Combat in the Cognitive Age** — Strategic context + positioning.
3. **Multi-Intelligence Arena** — Human + AI under constitutional rules.
4. **Why Structure Matters** — Constitutional and provenance framing.

## Design System Notes

- Generous negative space.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.
- Subtle, purposeful micro-interactions.
- Consistent iconography language (minimal, line-based).
- Dark mode friendly by default.
