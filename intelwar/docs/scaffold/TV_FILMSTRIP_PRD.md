**Version**: 1.0 · **Date**: July 19, 2026 · **Status**: Implementation archive

## Vision
Visual and interactive theatre of Intellectual Warfare — navigable recursive filmstrip.
Goals: (1) watchable high-rigor combat (2) recursive branching + in-situ forking (3) 0dentity merit.

## Concepts
Scene · Filmstrip · Branch · Fork · Heat · 0dentity Merit

## UX
Horizontal = time · Depth = recursion · Fork = sideways · Breadcrumbs · Go Deeper · Root return

## Scene production
Open hook 3–8s · Intellectual core · Close hook 3–8s · Ideal 45–75s · Fork-ready

## Metrics
AI autotagging · Heat composite · Bubbling topics · Emerging contested territories · Campaign heat map

## Merit
Not audit / social credit · Portable via 0dentity · Legible surfacing · Critical-node signals

## Modes
Live (emerging strip, limited forks) · Async (full recursion)

## Living Log
Filmstrip = experiential layer · Log = permanent provenance-backed record

## Out of scope v1
Decentralized multiplayer hosting · Reputation financialization · Automated truth scoring · Unrelated social networking
